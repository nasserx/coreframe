# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Coreframe's maintainers and contributors are committed to an open, welcoming,
inclusive, and healthy community. Participation should be free from harassment
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Constructive participation includes:

- treating other people with empathy and respect;
- welcoming different viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and receiving specific, constructive technical feedback;
- taking responsibility for mistakes, repairing harm where possible, and
  learning from the experience;
- focusing on the health of the project and community rather than personal
  advantage.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- sexualized language, imagery, attention, or advances;
- trolling, insults, derogatory comments, or personal and political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith;
- conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  community.

## Maintainer responsibilities

The repository maintainer is responsible for clarifying and applying these
standards and may take proportionate corrective action when behavior is
inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues,
pull requests, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of
Conduct. Reasons for moderation decisions will be communicated when doing so is
appropriate and does not compromise anyone's privacy or safety.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in repository Issues, pull requests, Discussions,
code review, other project-managed spaces, and public spaces where someone is
officially representing Coreframe.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer using a private
contact method made available through the maintainer's GitHub profile:
<https://github.com/nasserx>. Do not place incident details or another person's
private information in a public Issue, pull request, or Discussion.

Before the repository accepts public participation, the maintainer must verify
that the profile exposes a working private contact method. If no suitable
maintainer channel is available for conduct occurring on GitHub, use GitHub's
platform abuse-reporting tools and preserve relevant links or evidence.

Reports will be reviewed as privately, promptly, and fairly as practical. No
particular response time or outcome is guaranteed. The maintainer will protect
the reporter's privacy and safety to the extent reasonably possible, while
recognizing that a fair investigation may require sharing limited information
with affected people.

## Enforcement guidelines

The maintainer will consider context, severity, impact, intent, and patterns of
behavior when applying consequences.

### 1. Correction

For isolated unprofessional or unwelcome behavior, the maintainer may provide a
private explanation and request a correction or apology.

### 2. Warning

For a significant incident or repeated inappropriate behavior, the maintainer
may issue a formal warning and set conditions on further participation or
contact.

### 3. Temporary restriction

For a serious violation or continued behavior after a warning, the maintainer
may temporarily restrict participation and prohibit contact with affected
people or those enforcing this Code of Conduct.

### 4. Permanent restriction

For sustained harassment, aggression, disparagement of groups, or a repeated
pattern of serious violations, the maintainer may permanently prohibit public
participation in Coreframe community spaces.
